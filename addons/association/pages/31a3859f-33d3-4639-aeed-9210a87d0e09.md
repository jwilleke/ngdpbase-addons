# Using Association

The __association__ add-on is the unit owners association's own part of the site. Today it runs one
thing: the Occupancy information form, which Ohio Revised Code 5311.09 asks the association to keep
for each unit owner, and its review by the association. It works on top of the __accommodation__
add-on, which keeps the units and who owns and lives in them (see [Using Accommodation]). It ships
with this page and the [Occupancy Information] page, which are added to [{$applicationname}] when the
add-on is turned on.

## How it works

1. __An owner fills in the form__ on [Occupancy Information]: who they are, their unit, the date they
   became an owner, their addresses and phones, an agent if they have one, and an emergency contact.
   They do not need to be signed in. The form is for owners only.
2. __The association reviews it.__ Nothing changes on the site until a reviewer approves it. The
   reviewers are told a form is waiting; the notice names the unit, not the person.
3. __On approval__, the owner is added to their unit, their details are saved to their profile, and,
   if they have no account yet, one is made and they are emailed an invitation to set it up.
4. __The owner then signs in__ and, from My accommodation, adds the people who live in the unit and
   where its invoices go.

Occupants never use this form: their unit's owner adds them.

## For a reviewer

Open __Admin → Occupancy review__ (`/addons/association/review`). It lists the forms waiting, with
their answers. For each one:

- __Approve__ when it is right. If the person has no account, give a username for the new one. For an
  owner, check the share (100% for a sole owner; joint owners split it).
- __Reject__ when it should not be applied. Nothing is changed.

If something cannot be applied (a wrong unit, a bad date), the page says why and the form stays
waiting.

## For an owner

- __New owner__, or your information changed: fill in [Occupancy Information]. Answer whether you are
  already an owner in the association. An owner of more than one unit fills it in for each.
- __After approval__: sign in (use the invitation email if you are new) and open My accommodation to
  add your occupants and your billing address.

## Things to know

- What a form holds is kept in the add-on's own encrypted store, for the association only.
- Each submission, approval and rejection is recorded, naming who did it, never the answers.
- Board members, officers and elections come later, in a separate add-on.

## For the site operator

The add-on needs accommodation turned on, the site's database on, and the database key set. The forms
add-on carries the form.

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.addons.accommodation.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.forms.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.association.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.database.provider": "sqlite"
}
```

Set `NGDPBASE_DATABASE_KEY` in the site's `.env` and keep a copy off the server; without it the add-on
does not start, and Admin → Add-ons says why. Reviewers hold `association-review`, which administrators
have; give it to the board in the site's own policy.
