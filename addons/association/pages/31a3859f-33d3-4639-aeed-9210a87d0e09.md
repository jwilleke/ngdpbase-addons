# Using Association

The __association__ add-on is the unit owners association's own part of the site. Today it runs one thing: the Occupancy information form, which Ohio Revised Code 5311.09 asks the association to keep for each unit owner, and its review by the association. It works on top of the __accommodation__ add-on, which keeps the units and who owns and lives in them (see [Using Accommodation]). It ships with this page and the [Occupancy Information] page, which are added to [{$applicationname}] when the add-on is turned on.

## How it works

- 1 __An owner fills in the form__ on [Occupancy Information]: who they are, their unit, the date they became an owner, their addresses and phones, an agent if they have one, and an emergency contact. They do not need to be signed in. The form is for owners only.
- 2 __The association reviews it.__ The form becomes a request waiting on the [Reviews] page and the board's [Executive Hub]. Nothing changes on the site until a reviewer approves it. The reviewers are told a request is waiting; the notice does not name the person.
- 3 __On approval__, the site applies it: the owner is added to their unit, their details are saved to their profile, and, if they have no account yet, one is made and they are emailed an invitation to set it up. The record says who approved it.
- 4 __The owner then signs in__ and, from My accommodation, adds the people who live in the unit and where its invoices go.

Occupants never use this form: their unit's owner adds them.

## For a reviewer

Each form waiting is on the [Reviews] page, on the [Executive Hub] for the board, and on __Admin → Occupancy review__ (`/addons/association/review`), which shows the answers in full. Deciding it on any of them is the same decision.

- __Approve__ when it is right. The site applies it for you, so approving needs only the right to review occupancy forms. An owner is given a 100% share; for joint owners, set the split on the unit's page afterwards. If the person has no account, one is made with a username from their name.
- __Reject__ when it should not be applied. Nothing is changed.

If something cannot be applied (a wrong unit, two accounts with the same email), the page says why and the form stays waiting; fix it and approve again.

## For the board

Anyone holding the executive role can read the forms waiting and approve or reject them, on the [Executive Hub], on [Reviews] or on Occupancy review. The hub has an __Occupancy review__ card that counts them. The board needs no other permission: when a board member approves, the site adds the owner and saves their details, and the record names the board member who approved.

## For an owner

- __New owner__, or your information changed: fill in [Occupancy Information]. Answer whether you are already an owner in the association. An owner of more than one unit fills it in for each.
- __After approval__: sign in (use the invitation email if you are new) and open My accommodation to add your occupants and your billing address.

## Things to know

- What a form holds is kept in the add-on's own encrypted store, for the association only.
- Each submission, approval and rejection is recorded, naming who did it, never the answers.
- Board members, officers and elections come later, in a separate add-on.

## For the site operator

The add-on needs accommodation turned on, the site's database on, and the database key set. The forms add-on carries the form.

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.addons.accommodation.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.forms.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.association.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.database.provider": "sqlite"
}
```

Set `NGDPBASE_DATABASE_KEY` in the site's `.env` and keep a copy off the server; without it the add-on does not start, and Admin → Add-ons says why. Reviewers hold `association-review`, which administrators and the executive role have.
