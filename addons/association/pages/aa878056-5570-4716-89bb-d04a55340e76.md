# Occupancy Information

Ohio Revised Code 5311.09 asks the association to keep contact information for each unit owner. This form is for owners: fill it in when you become an owner, or when your information changes.

What you send is kept for the association to review before anything is changed. Once it is approved, sign in and use My accommodation to add your unit's occupants and its billing address.
