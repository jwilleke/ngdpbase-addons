# Using Accommodation

The __accommodation__ add-on keeps the site's units (condo units, houses, apartments), who owns each one, who lives in each one, and where each unit's invoices are sent. The accounting add-on reads it to send dues invoices. It ships with this page, which is added to [{$applicationname}] when the add-on is turned on.

## What it keeps

- __Units__, each known by its county parcel number and shown by its street address, for example "13 FAIRWAY DR (66-09960.017)".
- __Owners and occupants__, each a person with an account on this site, with the date they started and, once they leave, the date they left. A unit's past owners and occupants stay as its history.
- __Each owner's share__ of the unit. Joint owners split it, for example 50% each; the shares always add up to 100%. The share is what each owner owes of every invoice.
- __Billing__: where the unit's invoices are sent. Each address is typed the way it goes on the envelope, names first, with its share of the invoice. Every unit must have at least one.
- __The county's record__ of the unit: the owner the county names, the mailing address it uses, and the date of the newest sale. It is shown for checking; it never replaces the site's own entries.

## Who uses it

| Who | Where | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator or treasurer | Admin → Owners and occupants | Everything below, for every unit |
| An owner | My accommodation | See their unit; set its billing; add, change and end its occupants |
| An occupant | My accommodation | See their unit and its owners' names |
| The board (the executive role) | [Executive Hub] and Admin → Owners and occupants | See every unit, its owners, occupants and billing; approve or reject owners' requests to change a unit's owners. They change nothing else |

The board is told of every change an owner makes.

## For the administrator or treasurer

Open __Admin → Owners and occupants__ (`/addons/accommodation/residents`). It lists every unit by street, with its owners and occupants today. Units marked __no billing address__ cannot be invoiced yet; the count at the top says how many. Fix those before the month's dues are prepared.

Click a unit to open its page. From top to bottom:

1. __County's record__: what the county says about the unit. Use it to check the owners and the billing address.
2. __Billing__: where invoices go.
   - Type each address in its box, names first, one line each for the street and the town.
   - One address: leave the share empty, it is 100%. More than one: give each a share, adding up to 100%.
   - To take an address off, empty its box. A unit must keep at least one.
   - A unit with none yet shows a suggestion from the county's record. Check it, change it if needed, and click __Save billing__.
3. __Owners and occupants__: one row per person. Click __Edit__ on a row to change it:
   - __Moved out or sold__: set __Through__ to their last day, and Save.
   - __Wrong dates or share__: change them, and Save.
   - __An occupant who is really an owner__ (or the other way): change __As__, give an owner a share, and Save.
   - __Entered by mistake__: click Remove. Do not remove someone who really lived there or owned it: end them instead, so the history stays.
4. __Add a person__: type their name.
   - Pick them from the list if they already have an account.
   - Anyone else gets an account made for them in the same save, with no password. No email is sent unless you give their email and tick the invitation box.
   - Choose owner or occupant. __From__ starts as the county's newest sale date; change it if needed.
   - An owner needs a share. For joint owners, give each their part.

A sale is a few steps: end the old owners on their last day, then add the new owners from the next day. Until both are done, the page warns that the shares do not add up to 100%.

## For the board

The [Executive Hub] has an __Owners and occupants__ card for anyone holding the executive role. It shows how many owners' requests to change a unit's owners are waiting, how many units have no current owner, and how many have no usable billing address and so cannot be invoiced. The card opens Admin → Owners and occupants, where the board can read every unit. An owner's request to change the owners is listed on the Executive Hub with the other requests waiting for the board; approving it applies the change. Board members are also told whenever an owner changes their unit's billing or occupants.

## For an owner

Sign in and open __My accommodation__ (`/addons/accommodation/mine`). For each unit you own:

- __Billing addresses__: open it to see where your unit's invoices go, change an address, add another or take one off. With more than one, give each a share adding up to 100%.
- __Occupants__: add the people who live in your unit, change their phones and emergency contact, or end their stay when they move out. Someone new gets an account and an email inviting them to set it up.

Each change applies at once, and the board is told. Adding or removing an owner is not done here: it needs the association's approval.

Your bills, payments and balance are on your profile, in the dues card the accounting add-on adds.

## For an occupant

Sign in and open __My accommodation__ to see your unit and its owners. Your own phones and contact details are on your profile.

## Plugins

## For the site operator

Turn the add-on on in the site's configuration:

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.addons.accommodation.enabled": true
}
```

Discovery alone does not enable an add-on; the key is required. The units' categories and their assessments are site settings (`ngdpbase.addons.accommodation.categories`); see the add-on's README.
