# Using Accommodation

This page ships with the __accommodation__ addon and is seeded into [{$applicationname}]
when the addon is enabled.

## Enable the addon

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.addons.accommodation.enabled": true
}
```

Discovery alone does not enable an addon — the key above is required.

## What it gives you

- __Owners and occupants__ (Admin, `/addons/accommodation/residents`): every accommodation by its street
  address, with its owners and occupants today. Accommodations that cannot be invoiced are marked
  "no billing address". Open one to:
  - see the county's record of it: its owner, mailing address and newest sale
  - set its billing: where its invoices go and each address's share
  - add a person as owner or occupant (someone without an account gets one, with no password and no
    email unless you ask), and edit or remove an entry
- __My accommodation__ (`/addons/accommodation/mine`): a signed-in owner or occupant sees their own
  unit. An owner also sets its billing addresses and adds, changes and ends its occupants.

Every unit must have a billing address before it can be invoiced. Who owes is always the unit's
owners on the invoice date, by their shares.
